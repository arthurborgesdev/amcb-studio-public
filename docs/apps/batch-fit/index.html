BatchFit Support

BatchFit converts, resizes and renames batches of JPEG, HEIC and PNG images while creating separate local copies.

Quick answers

  • Your original images are never replaced or edited.
  • Images are processed on-device and are not uploaded to us.
  • The first version supports up to 100 JPEG, HEIC or PNG images in one batch.
  • Capture dates, orientation and safe embedded metadata are carried to the matching output when the destination format supports them.
  • Lifetime access can be recovered from the purchase screen with Restore Purchase.

Supported scope

BatchFit handles still JPEG, HEIC and PNG images selected from Photos or Files. Video, Live Photos, RAW, animated GIF, SVG, PDF and proprietary metadata fields are outside the first version’s scope.
