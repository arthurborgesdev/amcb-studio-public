PDF Target Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

Summary

PDF Target does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. PDF documents selected in the app are processed locally on the device and are not uploaded to AMCB Studio.

Documents and generated files

The app temporarily copies a selected PDF into its private container to perform compression and creates a separate output file for sharing. The original file is not modified. Temporary files remain under operating-system storage management and may be removed by the system or when the app is deleted.

Purchases and local preferences

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. PDF Target reads the verified App Store entitlement needed to unlock lifetime access. The app also stores the number of successful free uses in preferences accessible only to the app. AMCB Studio does not operate a user account or purchase-tracking server for PDF Target.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

PDF Target includes no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or cross-app tracking technology.

Children

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the new practice is introduced.
