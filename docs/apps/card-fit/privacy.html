CardFit Privacy Policy

Effective August 19, 2026.

Contact files

CardFit processes only the VCF files you explicitly select in the system Files picker. Parsing, validation and export occur locally on your device. CardFit does not request access to the Contacts database, upload contact files or modify source files.

Data collection

CardFit does not collect, transmit, sell, share or use personal data for tracking. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional CardFit Lifetime purchase. CardFit receives only the entitlement status required to unlock the feature; Apple’s privacy policy governs purchase and account data.

Files you create

Exported ZIP packages are stored or shared only where you choose through Apple’s system share sheet. You are responsible for protecting those files after export.

Contact

For privacy questions, email arthurborges.dev@gmail.com.
