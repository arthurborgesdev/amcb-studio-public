XML Lens Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-20.

Summary

XML Lens does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or user accounts.

Files and processing

XML files selected in the app are read and processed entirely on the device. They are not uploaded to Arthur Borges or any third party. Source files stay unchanged. Export files remain on the device until the user chooses where to share or save them through the system share sheet.

Purchases

Lifetime access is processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process purchase and account information under Apple’s own privacy policy. XML Lens receives only the entitlement status needed to unlock the feature.

Data retention

XML Lens does not maintain a server-side database. Temporary export files are created in the app’s local temporary storage and may be removed by iOS.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the new practices take effect.

For privacy questions, email arthurborges.dev@gmail.com.
