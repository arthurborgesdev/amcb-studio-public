ExposureFit Support

ExposureFit estimates reflected-light exposure from camera metadata and turns a reading into equivalent ISO, aperture and shutter combinations.

Before relying on a reading

ExposureFit is an estimator, not a calibrated laboratory instrument. Compare it with a trusted meter before critical or unrepeatable work. Very dark scenes, unusual lighting and camera hardware differences can affect results.

Camera access

Camera access is required for metering. ExposureFit does not save camera frames, request location or upload readings. If the camera is unavailable, close other camera apps and confirm access in Settings.

Purchases
