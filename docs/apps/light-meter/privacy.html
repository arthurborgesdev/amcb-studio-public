ExposureFit Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-20.

Data collection

ExposureFit does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. The app has no account system, analytics, advertising SDK or backend.

Camera and measurements

The camera is used only on the device to estimate reflected-light exposure. ExposureFit reads exposure metadata supplied by iOS. It does not save camera frames, request location or upload measurements.

Local storage

The app stores only the local flag used to remember whether the free measurement card has been consumed. Shared measurement cards leave the app only when you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. ExposureFit receives entitlement status but not your payment-card details.
