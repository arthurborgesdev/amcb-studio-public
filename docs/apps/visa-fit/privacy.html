VisaFit Privacy Policy

VisaFit is designed so document photos do not need to leave your device.

Data collected by AMCB Studio

VisaFit does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or use personal data for tracking. The app contains no AMCB Studio account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or remote photo-processing service.

Photos and camera

When you choose a photo, Apple’s system picker provides a local copy to VisaFit. When you use the camera, the captured image is processed locally. On-device Apple frameworks measure technical and visible properties such as file dimensions, face count and position, exposure, and background variation. VisaFit does not identify the person in a photo.

Exports

When requested, VisaFit creates temporary local files for sharing: a PDF preflight report and the byte-identical original data selected by you. You control the destination through Apple’s share sheet. Temporary files may remain in system-managed storage until iOS removes them.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and restores. VisaFit receives only the entitlement state needed to unlock lifetime access. AMCB Studio does not receive your payment-card details.

Official-source links

Opening a government source leaves VisaFit and is governed by that website’s privacy practices. No photo is included in those links.

Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the change is released. Questions may be sent to arthurborges.dev@gmail.com. Do not email identity photos or confidential documents.
