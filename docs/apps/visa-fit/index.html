VisaFit Support

A private, validation-only preflight for passport and visa photos.

Important: VisaFit provides preparation guidance, not government certification. It never guarantees acceptance and is not affiliated with any authority.

How it works

  1. Select the document flow and open its official source.
  2. Choose the exact photo you intend to submit or take a new photo.
  3. Review pass, attention, fail, and human-check items.
  4. With lifetime access, export a PDF report beside the untouched original.

Privacy and photo handling

Analysis happens on your device. VisaFit has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or photo-upload service. The original selected by you is not retouched or silently recompressed.

Purchase support

Your first complete analysis is free. A single non-consumable purchase unlocks unlimited analyses and report exports. If access is missing after reinstalling, open the paywall and choose Restore purchases while signed into the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
