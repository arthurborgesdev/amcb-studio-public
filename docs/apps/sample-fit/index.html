SampleFit Support

SampleFit batch-conforms uncompressed WAV and AIFF samples to a precise PCM format while creating separate local copies.

Quick answers

  • Your original audio files are never replaced, edited or deleted.
  • Audio is processed on-device and is not uploaded to us.
  • The first version supports up to 100 mono or stereo PCM WAV and AIFF files in one batch.
  • Output options are 44.1 or 48 kHz, 16 or 24-bit, mono or stereo, in WAV or AIFF.
  • Generated filenames contain the chosen format and remain unique inside the batch.
  • Lifetime access can be recovered from the purchase screen with Restore Purchase.

Supported scope

SampleFit handles uncompressed PCM WAV and AIFF audio selected with Apple’s Files picker. Compressed codecs, multichannel audio, trimming, effects, tagging, mastering and manufacturer-specific transfer protocols are outside the first version’s scope.
