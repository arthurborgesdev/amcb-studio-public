EPUB Proof Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-24.

EPUB Proof does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Files and reports

Selected EPUB files are read locally through Apple’s document picker. Validation findings, exported PDF and JSON reports, and saved report history remain on the device unless you explicitly share or move them using Apple’s system interfaces. EPUB Proof never uploads the selected book to AMCB Studio.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. EPUB Proof receives only the entitlement state needed to unlock lifetime access; AMCB Studio does not receive your payment credentials.

Diagnostics

The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Apple may provide aggregated App Store diagnostics according to your device and App Store privacy settings.

For privacy questions, email arthurborges.dev@gmail.com.
