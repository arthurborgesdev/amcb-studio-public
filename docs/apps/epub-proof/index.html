EPUB Proof Support

EPUB Proof inspects a practical subset of EPUB 2 and EPUB 3 structure on iPhone and iPad. It creates PDF and JSON reports without changing or uploading the source book.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm the selected file ends in .epub and opens in Apple Books.
  • For purchase issues, use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
  • EPUB Proof is an explanatory preflight tool; it does not claim complete EPUBCheck conformance.

Email arthurborges.dev@gmail.com. Include the app version, device model and the finding code shown by the app. Do not attach confidential books.
